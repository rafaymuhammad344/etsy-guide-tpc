Chapter 1

Why Etsy is the easiest way to earn in dollars

Etsy is a global marketplace with millions of buyers, mostly in the US, UK and Europe. They pay in dollars, pounds and euros. When you convert that to rupees, even small sales add up fast.

The myth: "Etsy doesn't work in Pakistan."
The truth: Pakistan is officially supported on Etsy through Payoneer, and your earnings can be withdrawn to your local bank account.

Most people never start because they believe the myth. That's good news for you: less competition from Pakistan.
